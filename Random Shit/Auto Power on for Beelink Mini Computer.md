
Auto Power On for Beelink mini computers 

1. Press the Del key repeatedly after powering on the mini PC to enter the bios 
2. Use arrows to goto Chipset page 
3. Select PCH-IO Configuration, press enter 
4. Select State After G3 
5. Select S0 State 
6. Hit F4 and save 
7. Unplug mini pc and plug it back in to make sure that when power is connected it turns on
